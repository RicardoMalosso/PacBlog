This is the beginning of my personal corner of the web: a place to collect the things I build, write about what I learn, and make room for music.

## What lives here

- **Portfolio:** software projects and the ideas behind them.
- **Blog:** development notes and longer pieces.
- **Music:** listening notes, lyrics, and translations with context.
- **Experiments:** small things you can try right in your browser.

The site is available in Brazilian Portuguese and English. The language switch takes you to the matching page when a translation exists.

## Built to stay simple

All pages are generated from files in the repository and hosted on GitHub Pages. There is no server or database to maintain. Articles start as Markdown; interactive pieces use React.
